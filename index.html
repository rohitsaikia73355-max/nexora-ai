What is NEXORA AI?

NEXORA AI is a personal project to build an AI super agent: one place where you can talk to an assistant, review your websites and get help with content. It is designed around a simple promise: it only works with websites you own or are authorized to manage, and it never pretends a feature is connected when it isn’t.
